What is Cold Chain Logistics?

The cold chain is the uninterrupted sequence of refrigerated production, storage and distribution activities, along with associated equipment and logistics, which maintain a desired temperature range. At Horizon Logistics London, our temperature control begins the moment cargo is collected and is strictly safeguarded until handover at its final destination.

Whether moving delicate pharmaceutical serums, diagnostic lab specimens, vaccines, or perishable frozen meat and dairy, a single thermal breach can destroy the efficacy of products. We equip every vehicle with calibrated thermal data loggers, giving your business complete audit traceability.

Continuous Telemetry:

Live temperature readouts visible from in-cab monitor & dispatch office.

Printed Audit Trail:

Hard-copy thermal logs printed on delivery for regulatory compliance.
