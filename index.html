Logistics Services London FAQs

Essential information regarding London haulage, temperature-controlled transit, and same-day delivery logistics.

Deciding on a London logistics company to partner alongside should be given due consideration. Aligning with the right logistics business can resolve a considerable amount of your supply chain problems. Key topics of consideration include the pre-existing infrastructure and fleet capabilities, reputation, driver vetting, and ability to integrate with your established technology. At Horizon Logistics London, we prioritize responsiveness, precision, and over 50 years of collective industry experience.

Of all the haulage companies London offers, Horizon Logistics London boasts one of the finest reputations for comprehensive service offerings and unparalleled client satisfaction. Haulage in London requires deep expertise navigating Congestion Charge zones, ULEZ compliance, tight delivery windows, and multi-drop routes. Our account managers and 24/7 controllers ensure seamless distribution without guesswork or delays.

Our modern fleet of refrigerated vans is equipped with dual-evaporator cooling units maintaining strict thermal regimes from -25°C to +25°C. Every vehicle features continuous digital telemetry logging. Customers receive an electronic audit trail and printed temperature readouts upon delivery—vital for pharmaceuticals, vaccines, dairy, meat, and perishable catering.

For urgent same-day courier requests within Greater London and surrounding business parks, our average collection time is between 30 and 60 minutes. Once loaded, the vehicle travels directly to the delivery destination with zero intermediate co-loading unless specified.
