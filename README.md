# Horizon Logistics London - Redesigned Website

A modern, high-performance, and SEO-optimised web presence for **Horizon Logistics London (HLL)**, London's trusted temperature-controlled courier and haulage provider.

## 🚀 Key Improvements & Modern Enhancements

### 1. Modern & Professional Visual Design
- **Refined Brand Palette**: Professional Midnight Navy (`#0B1325`), Electric Freight Blue (`#1E60F2`), Ice Cyan (`#00B4D8`), and Pure White.
- **Official Brand Assets**: Uses the authentic high-resolution **HLL Horizon Logistics London** truck speed logo, genuine fleet photography, and custom service icons.
- **Fluid & Accessible Typography**: Powered by Google Fonts `Plus Jakarta Sans` with optimal contrast ratios meeting WCAG AAA accessibility standards.
- **Responsive Layout**: Designed mobile-first with a smooth off-canvas navigation drawer, interactive desktop dropdown menus, and a sticky mobile quick-action bar (1-tap 24/7 call & instant WhatsApp booking).

### 2. High-Utility Interactive Tools
- **Instant Quote & Dispatch Estimator**: Calculates real-time estimated rates and service level agreements (SLAs) based on vehicle size, route, and cargo type, with 1-click WhatsApp pre-filled booking.
- **Interactive Temperature Controller**: Showcase for thermal capabilities (-25°C to -18°C Deep Frozen, +2°C to +8°C GDP Pharma & Chilled Food, +15°C to +25°C Controlled Ambient).
- **Searchable Coverage Directory**: Organised by London Boroughs (Camden, Kensington, Westminster, Harrow, Wembley, Heathrow, etc.), Home Counties, and Major UK Cities.
- **Fleet Specifications Matrix**: Breakdown of small vans, LWB refrigerated vans, Luton box vans with tail lift, and HGV trucks.
- **Driver Recruitment & Application Portal**: Step-by-step driver onboarding form for couriers and commercial driver hire.

### 3. Google SEO & Technical Optimisation
- **Rich Schema.org JSON-LD**:
  - `LogisticsService` & `LocalBusiness` structured data
  - `GeoCoordinates` (Wembley, HA0 3FF)
  - `OpeningHoursSpecification` (24/7 round-the-clock availability)
  - `FAQPage` Schema for Google Rich Snippets
- **Targeted Semantic Metadata**: Optimized title tags, high-intent meta descriptions (150-160 characters), Open Graph (`og:*`), and Twitter Cards on all pages.
- **Blazing Fast Performance**: Zero bulky framework dependencies, native vanilla CSS & JavaScript, instant loading speed with zero layout shift (CLS 0).
- **SEO Infrastructure**: Valid `sitemap.xml` and `robots.txt` ready for Google Search Console.
